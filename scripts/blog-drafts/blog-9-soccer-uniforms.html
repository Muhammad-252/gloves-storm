Fabric and construction

  • Fabric: 140–160 gsm interlock or mesh polyester. It is light, wicks sweat away from the skin and dries quickly between matches.
  • Ventilation: breathable mesh zones on the side seams help keep players cool in summer tournaments.
  • Collar: crew neck, V-neck or a polo collar, in the same or a contrast color.
  • Shorts: matching shorts with an elastic waistband and inner drawcord, sublimated to match the jersey.
  • Socks: knitted with your colors and logo, with a cushioned sole.

Names, numbers, crests and sponsors

Our soccer kits are fully sublimated: the design is printed onto transfer paper and then heat-pressed so the ink becomes part of the fabric. That has three practical benefits for a club:

  • No limit on colors. A design with ten colors and gradients costs the same as a plain two-color kit.
  • Nothing to crack or peel. Numbers and sponsor logos last as long as the shirt does.
  • Every shirt can be different. Each player gets their own name and number in the same order, at no extra charge.

If you prefer a raised crest, we can add a 3D silicone heat-transfer badge or an embroidered patch instead of a printed crest. These are quoted per design.

Goalkeeper kits

Under the Laws of the Game (Law 4), goalkeepers must wear colors that stand out from the outfield players and the referee, so most clubs order a separate goalkeeper design. Our padded goalkeeper kits are long-sleeved, with padding at the elbows and hips, and cost $27 a set at 30–99 pieces. A goalkeeper kit counts as its own design, so include it when you ask for your quote.

Sizing for youth and adult teams

Kits are graded from youth sizes up to adult 4XL, and you can mix sizes freely within your 30 pieces, for example 10 youth medium, 10 youth large and 10 adult small. Sizes 4XL and above carry a 10% surcharge because they use more fabric. If you are unsure, order the sample in the size most of your players wear and check the fit before bulk production.

How to order custom soccer uniforms

  1. Send your crest, colors and any sponsor logos. We reply within 48 hours with a free digital mockup and an exact quote.
  2. Order a sample. A kit sample costs $80 all-in, including express courier. It is made in 5–7 days and arrives in 4–7 working days.
  3. Send your roster. A simple list of sizes, names and numbers is all we need.
  4. Approve and pay 50%. Production takes 2–3 weeks after you approve the sample.
  5. Pay the balance and receive your kits. They ship by DHL or FedEx in 4–7 working days, with door-to-door DDP delivery available on request.
