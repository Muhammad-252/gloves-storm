How a football jersey is built

  • Pro cut for pads: the shoulders and sleeves are shaped to fit over shoulder pads without riding up.
  • Reinforced shoulders: a heavier 250–280 gsm dazzle fabric on the shoulder yoke and a double-layer cowl take the wear from contact.
  • Mesh body: a breathable polyester mesh front and back keeps players cooler under their pads.
  • Stretch panels: four-way stretch side panels and cuffs give a tight fit that is harder for opponents to grab.
  • Bar-tacked seams: extra stitching at the stress points so seams don't split mid-season.

Game pants

Our football pants are made from four-way stretch fabric with internal pockets that hold thigh and knee pads in place. You can choose belt loops or a drawcord waist, and add side stripes and logos to match your jerseys.

Many teams combine the two: a sublimated jersey design with tackle twill numbers. Ask for both in your quote and compare.

High school programs in the USA must follow National Federation rules, which cover jersey numbers and how clearly they contrast with the jersey color. Check the current rules on the NFHS website or with your league before you approve the design.

Sizing

Sizes can be mixed within each 30-piece order, from youth to adult. Sizes 4XL and above carry a 10% surcharge. Because pad sizes vary, we recommend trying the sample on a player wearing full pads before you approve bulk production.
